How to Play

  1. Choose a biome: Desert, Mountain, Snow, or Coast.
  2. A disaster, its direction, and a building mission are revealed.
  3. Choose your preparation time: 45, 60, or 90 seconds.
  4. Read the briefing, then place your houses and high-rises on the safest zones you can find.
  5. When time runs out, the disaster strikes! Watch the simulation play out.
  6. Score points for survival, smart placement, and mission completion.
